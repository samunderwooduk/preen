! Tracking parameters the AdGuard URL Tracking filter and the ClearURLs rules don't
! remove yet, in AdGuard's $removeparam syntax. Exceptions (@@) work too, for a
! parameter a site turns out to need.
!
! Preen knows the list by its first and last lines, so keep both as they are.
